# Peer Review — Fleet / wave MCTS engine (issue #23)

## Summary

The branch makes `MCTSEngine` fleet-capable: `_mcts_iteration` now takes N roots and sweeps
each phase across the whole fleet (select leaves → batched `outcomes` → partition → one
`evaluate_positions` → scatter → batched expansion → per-tree backpropagation), `select_ply`
becomes the fleet at N = 1 over its retained root, `select_ply_with_policy` is replaced by
`select_plies_for_training`, expansion is extracted into `_expand_leaves` and widened to a
single `apply_plies` call spanning every expanding leaf, and `SelfPlayCollector` is adapted to
the new training surface at width one. A new `tests/core/test_mcts_engine_fleet.py` (285 lines)
pins batching, slot alignment, terminal drop-out, slot independence, and the all-or-nothing
expansion contract.

All six plan steps are implemented, and the story was amended with the four superseding
decisions as Step 6 required. The plan does include a README-verification step (Step 6), and
`README.md` was updated accordingly. `pyright` reports **0 errors, 0 warnings**; `ruff check .`
reports **All checks passed!**; `pytest` is green at **229 passed** with `pytest examples` at
**102 passed** — matching the plan's stated exit criteria (the plan predicted 216; the extra 13
are the new fleet tests). No correctness defects were found in the fleet mechanics: slot
ordering, the value scatter, and the expansion offset walk are all sound. The findings below
are one diagnosability regression created by fleet-wide batching, plus documentation and
naming issues.

**Post-review.** All ten findings have been actioned; per-item detail is in the Resolution
column. `pyright`, `ruff check .` and `pytest` (229 passed) are all still clean afterwards.
Finding #10 is only partly resolved and is the one item still needing a decision: #24 and #30
were straightforward renames, but #31 (fleet tournament play) turns out to be a real consumer
of the plural *play* form that #23 dropped as having none, so it cannot simply be pointed at
the training method. That is recorded as an open question in #31's story rather than guessed at
here.
